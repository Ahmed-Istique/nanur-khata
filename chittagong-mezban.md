## Ingredients
- 2 kg beef (with bone and fat)
- 2 tbsp Mezban special spice mix
- 1 cup mustard oil
- 2 tbsp ginger paste
- 2 tbsp garlic paste
- 1 cup fried onions (beresta)


## Instructions
1. Marinate beef with all spices and mustard oil for 1 hour.
2. Cook on medium heat until meat is tender and oil separates.
3. Serve hot with steamed rice or Radhuni khichuri!
